Awarded a full-course Master’s Admissions Scholarship, covering 100% of tuition fees
Thesis (defended 17 Nov 2026): “Hierarchical Energy-Efficient Scheduling in Semiconductor Assembly and Test Hybrid Flow Shops”
  • Formulated a four-stage MILP (job sequencing, machine assignment, burn-in batching, tester speed selection) under a lexicographic tardiness–energy objective, and developed an Extended Teachers’ TLBO (E-TTLBO) with a feasibility-preserving batch-packing decoder.
  • Reduced energy consumption by 8.8–21.7% without increasing tardiness, with ≥80% lexicographic win rates against Lex-GA (17/18 configurations) and Simulated Annealing (16/18).
