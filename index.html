About

I am a researcher in operations research and production scheduling at the Logistics System Lab, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam National University – Ho Chi Minh City. I recently completed my Master of Engineering in Logistics and Supply Chain Management at Ho Chi Minh City International University (HCMIU) on a full-tuition admissions scholarship, defending my thesis on hierarchical energy-efficient scheduling in semiconductor assembly and test hybrid flow shops in November 2026 under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Phan Nguyen Ky Phuc.

My research builds mathematical optimization models (MILP) and metaheuristic algorithms for complex manufacturing and logistics systems, with a focus on sustainable and human-centric operations (Industry 5.0): energy-efficient scheduling, batching and speed selection, and worker fatigue and rest decisions in flexible job shops. I care about reproducible computational research and release code and benchmark instances on GitHub.

Before graduate school, I earned a B.Eng. in Logistics and Supply Chain Management from HCMUT under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Phan Thi Mai Ha, where my Six Sigma thesis received the highest score in my cohort and became a first-author Springer publication. I have worked as a process and operations engineering intern at Intel Vietnam, a research assistant at Saigon International University, and a teaching assistant for AI Applications in Logistics and Supply Chain Optimization. In summer 2026 I was a fully funded Taiwan Experience Education Programme (TEEP) research intern with Asst. Prof. Nguyen Thi My Hanh at the Global Logistics and Innovative Optimization Lab, Yuan Ze University, Taiwan.

I am seeking fully-funded PhD positions starting Fall 2027 in operations research, scheduling and optimization. Please feel free to contact me.

Selected Publications

  1. CMC
    Lexicographic Energy-Efficient Scheduling in Semiconductor Assembly and Test Hybrid Flow Shops
    Vo Thi Hoang Quanh, Phan Nguyen Ky Phuc, and Nguyen Thi My Hanh
    Computers, Materials & Continua, Sep 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML Code

    We study energy-efficient scheduling in semiconductor assembly and test hybrid flow shops. A four-stage mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) model integrates job sequencing, machine assignment, burn-in batching and tester speed selection under a lexicographic tardiness–energy objective. To solve realistic instances, we develop an Extended Teachers’ Teaching-Learning-Based Optimization (E-TTLBO) algorithm with a feasibility-preserving batch-packing decoder. E-TTLBO achieves at least 80% lexicographic win rates in 17 of 18 configurations against a lexicographic genetic algorithm and in 16 of 18 against simulated annealing, reducing energy consumption by 8.8–21.7% without increasing tardiness.

  2. IM
    How Does Green Perceived Value Drive Green Purchase Intention? The Parallel Mediating Roles of Green Trust and Customer Engagement
    Nguyen Van Dat, Pham Ngoc Duong, and Vo Thi Hoang Quanh
    Innovative Marketing, Sep 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Vietnam’s green consumption transition is accelerating under national policy pressure, yet actual purchasing behavior remains constrained by psychological barriers that price-focused explanations cannot resolve. This study examines how green perceived value and electronic word of mouth (eWOM) receptivity shape green product purchase intention through the mediating roles of green trust and customer engagement. Data from an online survey of 324 consumers in Ho Chi Minh City were analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling. Green trust was the strongest direct driver of purchase intention, followed by customer engagement, and green perceived value influenced intention exclusively through these two indirect pathways, confirming full mediation.

    Vo Thi Hoang Quanh and Phan Thi Mai Ha
