In human-centric (Industry 5.0) manufacturing, operators get tired, and fatigue affects processing times, quality and well-being. Most scheduling models either ignore fatigue or assume that idle time between operations is enough for recovery.

This ongoing project models **rest as an explicit decision** in the flexible job shop scheduling problem with worker assignment. It examines why fatigue-aware schedules cannot rely on incidental idle time alone, and how to design algorithms that jointly decide sequencing, machine and worker assignment, and rest breaks.
