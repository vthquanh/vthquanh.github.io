Through a four-month industrial project with a cold-rolled stainless-steel manufacturer in Vietnam, I applied the **Six Sigma DMAIC** framework to identify the key factors driving thickness variation in the cold-rolling process.

- **Increased thickness yield from 81.6% to 95.82%**, exceeding the 90% project target.
- The thesis was defended in English with a score of 9.6/10, the highest in the graduating cohort.
- Supported by an HCMUT Student Scientific Research Programme grant (SVKSTN-2023-CK-16).
