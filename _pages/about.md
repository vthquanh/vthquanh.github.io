I am a researcher in **operations research and production scheduling** at the Logistics System Lab, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam National University – Ho Chi Minh City. I recently completed my **Master of Engineering in Logistics and Supply Chain Management** at Ho Chi Minh City International University (HCMIU) on a full-tuition admissions scholarship, defending my thesis on _hierarchical energy-efficient scheduling in semiconductor assembly and test hybrid flow shops_ in November 2026.

My research builds **mathematical optimization models (MILP) and metaheuristic algorithms** for complex manufacturing and logistics systems, with a focus on **sustainable and human-centric operations** (Industry 5.0): energy-efficient scheduling, batching and speed selection, and worker fatigue and rest decisions in flexible job shops. I care about reproducible computational research and release code and benchmark instances on [GitHub](https://github.com/vthquanh).

Before graduate school, I earned a B.Eng. in Logistics and Supply Chain Management from HCMUT, where my Six Sigma thesis received the highest score in my cohort and became a first-author Springer publication. I have worked as a process and operations engineering intern at **Intel Vietnam**, a research assistant at Saigon International University, and a teaching assistant for _AI Applications in Logistics and Supply Chain Optimization_. In 2026 I was a fully funded **Taiwan Experience Education Programme (TEEP)** fellow at Yuan Ze University.
